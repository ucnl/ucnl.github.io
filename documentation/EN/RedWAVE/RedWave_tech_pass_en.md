## 1. GENERAL INFORMATION ABOUT THE PRODUCT

The RedWave underwater acoustic navigation system is designed to determine the geographic location (latitude, longitude, depth) of RedNode underwater acoustic navigation receivers. The navigation data are generated in the navigation receivers.

The system consists of:
- RedBase underwater acoustic buoys;
- at least one RedNode underwater acoustic navigation receiver;
- in the diver version, a RedNav diver's navigator or an Aquatab diver's tablet is used;

The RedBase underwater acoustic buoys are made in housings with excess buoyancy and contain satellite navigation signal receivers, with which they determine their own geographic location, which is relayed into the underwater acoustic channel.

An unlimited number of RedNode navigation receivers can simultaneously determine their own geographic location while submerged, receiving signals from four RedBase buoys that form a so-called floating long navigation base.

The RedNode underwater acoustic navigation receivers are made using a patented technology as monoblocks completely potted in polyurethane compound and are designed for operation in fresh and salt water (salinity level up to **38 PSU**) and in the temperature range **from -5 to +50 °C**. 
The maximum operating hydrostatic pressure is **30 bar**, which approximately corresponds to 300 meters of water column. The nominal accuracy of determining the geographic location (2DRMS) is 0.84 m, and the depth resolution is 0.1 m.

The underwater acoustic buoys have built-in lithium iron phosphate batteries; the navigation receivers are powered either by the carrier (ROV, AUV, etc.) or, in the diver version, by the built-in power source of the RedNav diver's navigator or the Aquatab diver's tablet.

## 3. MANUFACTURER'S WARRANTY. SERVICE LIFE
The warranty period for the supplied equipment kit is **24 (twenty-four) months** and is counted from the date of actual receipt of the devices by the customer, unless otherwise provided by the contract.
If, during the warranty period, defects or latent defects are found in the product (latent defects are defects that could not be detected during a check usual for the given product and were revealed only during operation), the Supplier shall eliminate them at its own expense by repairing the product or replacing it with a new one within a period of **no more than 30 (thirty) calendar days** and specified in the certificate of transfer of the product for repair.
The warranty period for the product is extended by the time during which the product was not used because of the identified defects. The time during which the product was not used is counted from the date of the written notification to the Supplier of the identified defects until the day of receipt of the product under the acceptance certificate after repair (replacement).
When the product is replaced, the warranty period for the product is counted anew from the date the parties sign the acceptance certificate after repair (replacement).
Delivery of the product for replacement work from the Customer's premises, and after the work has been completed to the Customer's warehouse, is carried out using the Customer's own resources.

The warranty obligations do not apply to the product in the case of:  
- Violation of the rules for operating the product;
- Electrical damage to the product caused by water ingress through open connectors, damaged cable insulation, etc., which resulted in its failure and occurred after it was handed over to the Customer;
- Mechanical damage to the product that caused its loss or failure and occurred after it was handed over to the Customer.

## 4. NOTES ON OPERATION AND STORAGE

### 4.1. Interchangeability
Monoblock devices (navigation receivers) are fully interchangeable electrically. 

### 4.2. Special storage requirements
* The use of organic solvents and/or aggressive acids and/or alkalis to remove contamination from the surface of the devices and their cables is not allowed. 
* Storage of the devices without rinsing in fresh water or while damp is not allowed

### 4.3 Special safety precautions during operation
The devices are not rescue or human life-support equipment.
Any work using the supplied equipment must be performed in strict compliance with the requirements of the Intersectoral occupational safety rules for diving/underwater operations, as well as the departmental and local regulations in force for the Customer governing diving operations.
