## FEATURES

* **High-quality polyurethane sheath**
* **Environment-resistant**
* **Load-bearing core**

## DESCRIPTION

The **uWire** extension cable with a UART-RS422 converter is designed to supply power and provide a data interface for Zima-B/Zima2-B and Zima2-R devices in their standard configuration. It can also be used with uWave/uWave Max modems.
The lower part of the cable has a sealed connector. The UART-RS422 interface converter is located next to the connector and is potted in polyurethane compound.
