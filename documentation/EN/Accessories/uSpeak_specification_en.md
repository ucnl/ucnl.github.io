## FEATURES

* **Resistant to any diving modes and depths**
* **Domestic development and production**
* **Patented design<sup>[*](#fn_asterisk)</sup>**
* **Ergonomic design**
* **Strong and durable**
* **Can be used as a replacement for DEMSh, OTS HotMic™, etc.**

## DESCRIPTION

**uSpeak** microphones are fully potted in a high-quality polyurethane compound. Simple and reliable, they have no closed air cavities. They withstand any diving and ascent modes.
It connects via a bronze M2 threaded connection, as do most counterparts.
The device is available in two form factors: standard and enlarged.
