## FEATURES

* **Complete absence of corroding elements**
* **Domestic development and production**
* **Patented design<sup>[*](#fn_asterisk)</sup>**
* **Ergonomic design**
* **Strong, durable, highly visible housing**
* **Loop for a cord or strap**

## DESCRIPTION

The **uPress** underwater switch is specially designed for use by divers. The device has no parts that require maintenance or are susceptible to corrosion.
It offers high reliability and a high mean time between failures compared with spring-loaded rod designs and piezo buttons.
