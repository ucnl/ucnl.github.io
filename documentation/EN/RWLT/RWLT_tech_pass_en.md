## 1. GENERAL INFORMATION ABOUT THE PRODUCT

The RWLT underwater acoustic navigation system is designed to determine
- the geographic location (latitude, longitude, depth) of a moving underwater object equipped with an RWLT Pinger underwater acoustic pinger
- the geographic location (latitude, longitude) of divers equipped with RedPhone-DX wireless voice communication stations 

The navigation data are generated in the surface navigation receiver.

The system consists of:
- RWLT GIB underwater acoustic buoys;
- at least one Bat&Link Box autonomous power supply and switching unit;
- at least one uNav RWLT navigation receiver;  
The kit may also include:
- one or more RWLT Pinger underwater acoustic pingers;
- one or more battery packs for powering the pinger;

The RWLT GIB underwater acoustic buoys are made in housings with excess buoyancy and contain satellite navigation signal receivers, with which they determine their own geographic location, an underwater acoustic receiving path and a radio-frequency transmitting path. Four RWLT GIB underwater acoustic buoys form a so-called floating navigation base: when the underwater acoustic signal from the RWLT Pinger is received at all elements of the navigation base, these elements transmit the navigation information to the uNav RWLT navigation receiver, which calculates the geographic location of the pinger. Similarly, in the case of RedPhone-DX wireless voice communication stations, the navigation signal is emitted at the moment the diver finishes transmitting a voice message. The system can operate in only one mode at a time.

The RWLT Pinger underwater acoustic pinger is made using a patented technology as a monoblock completely potted in polyurethane compound and is designed for operation in fresh and salt water (salinity level up to **38 PSU**) and in the temperature range **from -5 to +50 °C**. 
The maximum operating hydrostatic pressure is **30 bar**, which approximately corresponds to 300 meters of water column. The nominal accuracy of determining the geographic location (2DRMS) is 1.5 m.

The uNav RWLT navigation receiver is made as a monoblock completely potted in polyurethane compound. It has a built-in satellite navigation signal receiver and a radio-frequency receiving path.

The Bat&Link Box autonomous power supply and switching unit is made as a plastic case. Inside, it contains a power source based on LiFePO4 cells.

If, during the warranty period, defects or latent defects are found in the product (latent defects are defects that could not be detected during a check usual for the given product and were revealed only during operation), the Supplier shall eliminate them at its own expense by repairing the product or replacing it with a new one within a period of **no more than 30 (thirty) calendar days** and specified in the certificate of transfer of the product for repair.

## 4. NOTES ON OPERATION AND STORAGE

### 4.1. Interchangeability
Monoblock devices (pingers, navigation receivers) are fully interchangeable electrically.  
The navigation buoys have a fixed address (1 .. 4), and a buoy can be replaced only with a buoy with the same address. The buoy address is marked on the float.

### 4.2. Special storage requirements
* The use of organic solvents and/or aggressive acids and/or alkalis to remove contamination from the surface of the devices and their cables is not allowed. 
* Storage of the devices without rinsing in fresh water or while damp is not allowed

### 4.3 Special safety precautions during operation
The devices are not rescue or human life-support equipment.
Any work using the devices must be performed in strict compliance with the requirements of the Intersectoral occupational safety rules for diving/underwater operations, as well as the departmental and local regulations in force for the Customer governing diving operations.
