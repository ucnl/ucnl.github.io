## 1. Test object
The test object is the set of instruments and equipment included in the **RedPhone** wireless underwater acoustic voice communication system (hereinafter referred to as the equipment set, ES). 

## 2. Test objective
Determination of the compliance of the ES with the declared technical specifications.

## 3. General information
### 3.1. Instrument composition
The **RedPhone** wireless underwater acoustic voice communication system was developed by UCNL LLC; the base equipment set includes the following instruments:
- [RedPhone-OS](RedPhone_OS_Specification_en.md) - surface communication station;
- [RedPhone-D](RedPhone_Specification_en.md) - diver communication station.  

They are manufactured at the facilities of **UCNL LLC**.

### 3.2. Procedure for using additional equipment and transport cases
Additional equipment means any devices and accessories that are not an integral part of the ES instruments, in particular:
- diving masks, diving telephone headsets;
- additional light signaling equipment: retroreflective markers, reflectors, light beacons, lamps, etc.;
- various devices: resistance, temperature, voltage, pressure and depth meters;  

Transport cases are special cases for transporting the instruments included in the ES.
The additional equipment and transport cases are not manufactured by **UCNL LLC** and are not test objects under this test program and procedures; their composition is agreed with the customer separately. By separate agreement, it is also possible to carry out both delivery tests of a separate set of additional equipment and/or transport cases and their incoming inspection according to a procedure agreed separately with the customer.

### 3.3 Frequency of testing
Tests according to this procedure are carried out on one ES from each series of equipment. Additional tests are carried out when changes are made to the design of the instruments and/or to the specialized software of the ES.
By separate agreement, acceptance tests according to this procedure or according to a simplified scheme are possible.

## 5. Test site requirements
The test site for testing according to this test program and procedures must meet a number of requirements, in particular:
- fresh or salt water body;
- for intelligibility tests, the size of the water body is not less than **100 x 50 m** (testing in a pool is possible, if each of its dimensions is **not less than L x W x D - 25 x 25 x 2 m**);
- for maximum communication range tests, the size of the water body is not less than **200 x 1500 m**;
- sea state **no more than 0.5** (according to the meteorological service);
- no currents with a speed of more than **1 m/s**;
- water depth at the site **not less than 2** and **not more than 40 m**;
- line-of-sight conditions between the communicating devices must be ensured;
The test site must have free access to the water so that the divers can enter the water and the transducer of the surface communication station can be deployed without hindrance;
Preference is given to water bodies with a relatively flat bottom composed of sandy and/or silty soils and a depth of about **10–15 m**, with the size of the water body **not less than 100 x 100 m**.

### 6.2.1. Test procedures for the RedPhone-OS surface communication station
#### 6.2.1.1. External visual inspection
Perform this inspection with the station lid open. During the inspection, check that:

* The protective caps on the **"Speaker"** and **"Power"** toggle switches on the front panel of the station are intact;
* There is no water, salt deposits or other contamination inside the connectors on the front panel of the station and the mating connectors (transducer, microphone, headphones (if used));
* The insulation of the transducer and microphone cables is intact;

> **If the observed conditions do not match the expected ones, operation of the station is prohibited. The ES is considered to have failed the check**.

#### 6.2.1.2. Functional check of the device
Perform this check after a successful visual inspection in accordance with [section 6.2.1.1](#6211-external-visual-inspection).

Additional check conditions:
* the **"Power"** toggle switch is set to the **"Off"** position;
* the **"Speaker"** toggle switch is set to the **"On"** position; 
* the **"Volume"** control is set to the 12 o'clock position (50% of the scale);

During the check, make sure that:

* When the **"Power"** toggle switch is moved to the **"On"** position, the station emits a short beep, indicating that power is present and the device is operational;

[Sound example](/documentation/redphone_pwon.wav)  

After the check, move the **"Power"** toggle switch to the **"Off"** position.

> **If the observed conditions do not match the expected ones, operation of the station is prohibited. The ES is considered to have failed the check**.

#### 6.1.2.3. Checking the transducer and microphone
Perform this check after the successful checks in accordance with [section 6.2.1.1](#6211-external-visual-inspection) and [section 6.2.1.2](#6212-functional-check-of-the-device).

Additional check conditions:
* the **"Power"** toggle switch is set to the **"Off"** position;
* the **"Speaker"** toggle switch is set to the **"On"** position; 
* the **"Volume"** control is set to the **12 o'clock position (50% of the scale)**;
* the supplied **transducer** is connected to the **"Antenna"** connector on the front panel of the station;
* the supplied **microphone** is connected to the **"Microphone"** connector;
* the **PTT button** (_PTT, Push-to-talk_) on the microphone is **not pressed**;

During the check, make sure that:

* When the **"Power"** toggle switch is moved to the **"On"** position, the station emits a short beep, indicating that power is present and the device is operational;
* A constant background noise is heard from the station speaker, and its intensity may vary;
* When the transducer is touched, the station responds with a change in the noise (for example, when the transducer is tapped lightly, these clicks should be heard from the station speaker);
* When the **PTT button is pressed**, the noise from the speakers stops completely (transmission is in progress), and when it is **released**, the station emits a short beep, after which the background noise may be heard again;

After the check, move the **"Power"** toggle switch to the **"Off"** position.

[Sound example](/documentation/redphone_pwon_rx_check.wav)  

> **If the observed conditions do not match the expected ones, operation of the station is prohibited. The ES is considered to have failed the check**.

> To check the headphones, the **"Speaker"** toggle switch must be in the **"Off"** state and all sound signals, including the background noise, must be monitored through the headphones using the procedure described above.

### 6.3. Communication system operability test
The tests are carried out to reject obviously inoperable ES and their components.
The surface and diver stations have successfully passed the visual inspections and preparations in accordance with [section 6.2](#62-preparation-and-checking-of-the-equipment-for-testing).
A diver equipped with a RedPhone-D station dives to a shallow depth (~1–1.5 m) and moves away from the location of the RedPhone-OS station transducer to a distance of **10–15 m**.
Both the surface station operator and the diver are briefed in advance on the procedure for this check. The check proceeds as follows:  
- The surface station operator presses the _PTT_ button and, after a short pause (0.5 s), slowly says the names of the digits from 1 to 9 with clear articulation, ending the transmission with the word "Over!" and, after a short pause (0.5 s), releases the _PTT_ button;
- Then the diver makes the same transmission if the diver clearly heard the words transmitted from the surface; meanwhile, the surface post verifies that the voice message has been received.

> **If there is no communication after three unsuccessful attempts, the ES is considered to have failed the check**.


### 6.4. Intelligibility test
The tests are carried out to confirm the quality of communication.
The tests are carried out on at least three diver - surface communication station operator pairs.
If the participants have hearing levels of more than 90% confirmed by a specialized medical institution, it is permitted to conduct the test on one "diver - operator" pair, but the total number of words transmitted must be at least 200 (4 lists).

The test for one diver - operator pair is conducted according to the following procedure:
The diver is instructed to repeat each received word over the underwater telephone link.
The diver is positioned at a comfortable distance from the location where the transducer of the surface communication station is lowered, but not closer than 10 m and not farther than 1000 m.
The immersion depth in this case must be at least 2–3 m.
During the test, the relative position of the diver and the surface station must not change significantly (by more than 2–3 m horizontally).

The surface station operator says the words from the articulation lists in [section 6.4.1](#641-articulation-lists) in turn; each of them must be repeated by the diver, after which the word is crossed out (or marked) to avoid repetition.

When recording the results, only the exact reproduction by the diver of all phonemes of the heard word is accepted as a correct answer. Any change in the repetition of the word is regarded as an incorrect answer. 

The total number of check marks in the **"Without errors"** columns is counted and entered in the **"Number of error-free repetitions"** row.  
To determine the value of the **intelligibility percentage**, the value of the **"number of error-free repetitions"** is divided by the total number of words in the form and multiplied by **100%**. The resulting value is entered in the **"Intelligibility percentage"** field.
Then the **"Intelligibility percentage"** is averaged over all completed forms (summed and divided by the number of completed forms). The final value is called the **"Average intelligibility percentage"**.

> **The test is considered passed if the average intelligibility percentage based on the results of at least three tests is not lower than 70%.**

### 6.5. Maximum communication range test
The tests are carried out to confirm the declared maximum communication range.
At the test site, a location 1000 m away from the place where the surface station transducer is lowered is marked with an anchor and a buoy. The distance is checked either with a GNSS receiver or with a rangefinder.
At this location, the diver descends to a depth of at least 2–3 m and transmits voice messages, reporting readiness. During this test, the diver must not move away from the descent point by more than 2–3 m.

Short messages are transmitted in turn between the diver and the surface point. Next, in their replies, the diver and the station report a subjective assessment of communication on a 10-point scale. The recommended sequence of messages:

- **Diver:** *In position. How do you read me? Over*
- **Surface station:** *I hear you X out of 10. Understood. How are you feeling? Over*
- **Diver:** *Received. I feel fine. I hear you Y out of 10. Over*

And so on.

> **If speech messages are successfully received by the diver and by the surface station in at least one of 5 attempts, the ES is considered to have passed the test, and the declared characteristic is considered confirmed.**


### 6.6. Instrument battery life test
The instrument battery life test is carried out without the need for diving. The diver station is placed in a container of water so that the entire housing of the device is covered with water, while the cable with the connected headset remains in the air. The ambient temperature must remain within the range from 20 °C to 30 °C.
On the surface station, the **Speaker** toggle switch must be turned on and the volume set to the 12 o'clock position (50% of the scale).
The fully charged devices - the surface communication station and the diver station - are switched on and left for the declared operating time in receive mode: 
- 7 hours 45 minutes for the surface communication station
- 99 hours 45 minutes for the diver communication station. 

The time is measured with a clock or a timer.

After the specified time has elapsed (for each instrument separately), the instrument is switched off (the diver station is taken out of the water for this) and switched on again; the instrument must play the power-on sound, as described in the corresponding user's manual.

> **The ES is considered to have passed the test if the corresponding sound is played when both instruments are switched on again.**
