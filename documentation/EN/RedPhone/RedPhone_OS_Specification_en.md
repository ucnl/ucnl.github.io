## FEATURES

* **Compatible with most similar systems (OTS, Oceanreef, etc.)**
* **Acoustic communication range up to 1000<sup>[1](#footnote1)</sup> m**
* **Rugged, durable plastic housing**
* **Highly sensitive receiver**
* **Built-in voice activity detector**

## DESCRIPTION

The **RedPhone-OS** wireless diver telephone communication station provides voice message transmission between the surface station and the divers. 
The device supports standard frequency bands and is compatible with most similar systems. The device is housed in an impact-resistant 
plastic case, and the control panel is made of stainless steel with inscriptions applied by laser engraving. 
The built-in power supply consists of lithium iron phosphate batteries that operate at subzero temperatures
and provide more than **3000** charge-discharge cycles.
