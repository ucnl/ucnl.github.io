### KIT No. 1

| Name | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| [A³R](A3R_Datasheet_en.md): single-frequency receiver module  | 6 pcs |
| [A³T](A3T_Datasheet_en.md): single-frequency pulse transmitter module  | 2 pcs |
| [RT-1.332820-1](https://docs.unavlab.com/documentation/EN/Transducers/RT_1_332820_1_Specification_en.html): underwater acoustic transceiving transducer | 2 pcs |
| [R-1.d3505-1](/documentation/EN/Transducers/R_1.d3505_1_Specification_en): underwater acoustic receiving transducer | 5 pcs |

#### The kit allows you to assemble
- two transceivers + a 4-element receiving array
- 1 transceiver + a transceiver with a 5-element receiving array
- two transceivers + 4 receivers

#### What can you do with this kit?
- USBL/SBL
  - with propagation time measurement by the "request-response" method
  - with propagation time measurement to a pinger (for example, by prior synchronization)
- LBL
  - range-difference system - an analog of the [WAYU](/documentation/EN/WAYU/WAYU_DataBrief_en.html) system
  - seabed ranging base
- VLBL 
- two-way communication
- propagation time measurement by the "request-response" method
- propagation time measurement by prior synchronization

> Note: additional components may be required for assembly: GNSS receivers, microcontroller boards, etc., which are not included in the A³S delivery set
