### 2.3. Other hazards
The battery's constituent substances are contained in hermetically sealed cells designed to withstand the temperatures and pressures encountered under normal operating conditions. This design also provides protection against ignition or explosion during normal operation and prevents leakage of these substances.
If the battery housing is destroyed, the constituent substances are released.

## 7. RULES FOR STORAGE AND HANDLING OF THE CHEMICAL PRODUCT DURING LOADING AND UNLOADING
### 7.1. Precautions for safe handling
Do not allow the battery cells to become saturated with water (seawater).  
Do not expose to strong oxidizers.  
Do not throw. Avoid mechanical (impact) loads.  
Do not disassemble, modify the design or deform.  
Do not allow the positive and negative terminals to come into contact with electrically conductive material.  
Use only the specified chargers and tools for recharging and discharging.  
**Advice on industrial hygiene**
Wash hands before breaks and after work. Keep away from food, drinks and animal feed.

### 7.2. Conditions for safe storage, including any incompatibilities
Avoid exposure to direct sunlight, high temperatures and high humidity. Store in a cool place. Storage temperature: -20 to 60 °C. Permissible air humidity: 45–85 %.
**Incompatible substances or mixtures**  
Store separately from water.  
Do not store together with electrically conductive materials.  
**Other advice to consider**
Do not store in places subject to static electricity buildup.  
Protect from heat and direct sunlight.  
Protect from air humidity and water.  

### 7.3. Specific end use(s)
Use only for the intended purpose; see the user's manual.

## 8. EXPOSURE CONTROLS AND PERSONAL PROTECTION
### 8.1. Engineering controls:
Do not store batteries near sources of heat or open flames. Store in a dry, cold place.
### 8.2. Personal protective equipment:
**Respirator:** Not required during normal operation. In the event of a fire, self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) is required. Eye/face protection: Not required if safety rules are followed.  
**Gloves:** Not required when handling batteries.  
**Foot protection:** Not required

## 11. TOXICOLOGICAL INFORMATION
This product does not exhibit toxicity when handled and used correctly.
**Signs and symptoms:** None unless the battery is destroyed.
Excessive exposure may cause symptoms of damage to non-fibrous lung tissue and irritation of the mucous membranes.  
**Inhalation:** Lung irritation.  
**Skin contact:** Skin irritation  
**Eye contact:** Eye irritation  
**Ingestion:** If swallowed, damage to the tissues of the larynx and gastrointestinal tract.  
**Medical conditions generally aggravated by exposure:** Exposure to the internal contents of the battery may cause eczema, allergic skin reactions, lung damage, asthma and other respiratory diseases.
