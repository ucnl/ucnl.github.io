## 1. GENERAL INFORMATION ABOUT THE PRODUCT

The system is designed to enable the recovery of various autonomous bottom stations.
The system consists of three main devices:
- **F4105-SU** programming and command transmission unit. It is made as a plastic case with a stainless steel front panel on which the controls and connectors are located. The device contains a built-in power source based on LiFePO4 batteries. It is designed for setting (programming) one of the 64 possible addresses of the **F4105-AU** acoustic wake-up unit, as well as for issuing remote addressed commands to the wake-up units.
- **F4105-AU** acoustic wake-up unit. It is made as a monoblock with a cable and a watertight connector. The wake-up unit is an underwater acoustic receiver designed for long-term (up to 2 months) standby for a special underwater acoustic signal, the parameters of which are set when the wake-up unit is connected to the **F4105-SU** programming and control unit. Upon receiving the specified command, the wake-up unit generates a logic control signal, which is fed to the actuating device, the **F4105-BU** release unit, ensuring uncoupling of the device from the anchor and its ascent.
- **F4105-BU** release unit. The device contains a screw mechanism and a built-in power source based on LiFePO4 batteries and is designed for uncoupling from the anchor by a control signal coming from the control device (**F4104-AU**).

The maximum underwater acoustic communication range between the devices of the kit is 200 m. The release unit and the acoustic wake-up unit have a maximum immersion depth of 200 m.

## 3. MANUFACTURER'S WARRANTY. SERVICE LIFE
The warranty period for the supplied equipment kit is **24 (twenty-four) months** and is counted from the date of actual receipt of the devices by the customer, unless otherwise provided by the contract.
If, during the warranty period, defects or latent defects are found in the product (latent defects are defects that could not be detected during a check usual for the given product and were revealed only during operation), the Supplier shall eliminate them at its own expense by repairing the product or replacing it with a new one within a period of **no more than 30 (thirty) calendar days** and specified in the certificate of transfer of the product for repair.
The warranty period for the product is extended by the time during which the product was not used because of the identified defects. The time during which the product was not used is counted from the date of the written notification to the Supplier of the identified defects until the day of receipt of the product under the acceptance certificate after repair (replacement).
When the product is replaced, the warranty period for the product is counted anew from the date the parties sign the acceptance certificate after repair (replacement).
Delivery of the product for replacement work from the Customer's premises, and after the work has been completed to the Customer's warehouse, is carried out using the Customer's own resources.

The warranty obligations do not apply to the product in the case of:  
- Violation of the rules for operating the product;
- Electrical damage to the product as a result of use with third-party accessories (headsets, chargers, etc.);
- Mechanical damage to the product that caused its loss or failure and occurred after it was handed over to the Customer.

## 4. NOTES ON OPERATION AND STORAGE

### 4.1. Interchangeability
Devices of the same type are fully interchangeable electrically. 

### 4.2. Special storage requirements
The use of organic solvents and/or aggressive acids and/or alkalis to remove contamination from the surface of the devices and cables is not allowed.

### 4.3 Special safety precautions during operation
The devices are not rescue or human life-support equipment.
Any work using the devices must be performed in strict compliance with the requirements of the intersectoral rules for diving/underwater operations, as well as the departmental and local regulations in force for the Customer governing diving operations.
